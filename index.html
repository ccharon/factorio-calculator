What does clicking on an item's icon in the "Factory" tab do?

It omits the dependencies of that item from the solution. The intent behind this feature is to model the case of building a factory when you are shipping in one or more ingredients from elsewhere. For example, if you are making an advanced circuit factory and want to ship in electronic circuits, and want to see the remaining copper cable requirements on their own.

Clicking on the icon a second time will restore its dependencies.

How do I plan for several planets?

Click a planet in the settings to select only that planet. Shift-click adds or removes a planet. Recipes and buildings are available if they work on at least one selected planet.

Why is no power plant in the solution?

Electricity and heat are free imports by default. Enable a generator, such as the steam engine or the solar panel of a planet, in the recipe toggles to include it. While one is enabled, the factory has to produce its own electricity.

How do I plan for items of higher quality?

Choose the quality next to the target item, and put quality modules into the buildings, for example with the default module setting. Products of higher quality appear as separate rows with a quality badge. To recycle items for another chance at quality, enable their recycling recipes in the recipe toggles.

How do I change which resources the solver prefers?

In the "Resources" tab, drag a resource into another level. Resources at the top are used first. Within a level, a lower weight is preferred. With the keyboard, the left and right arrow keys select a resource and the up and down arrow keys move it.

Features:

  • Multiple simultaneous outputs, with automatic handling of recipes that produce several products, such as oil processing.
  • Modules and beacons.
  • Planet selection, which limits recipes and resources to what is available there.
  • Exact rational arithmetic, so results have no floating-point errors.
  • Rates per second, minute or hour.
  • Quality of machines, modules and beacons per recipe, and recipe productivity research with the productivity cap.
  • Targets of higher quality, solved with quality modules and recycling loops.
  • Space platforms with asteroid collection, crushing and thruster fuel, and rocket launches of every launchable item.
  • Power and heat: steam engines, solar panels per planet, nuclear and fusion power, heating towers.
  • Agricultural towers and burner buildings with a choice of fuel per fuel category.
